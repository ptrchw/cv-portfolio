About

Two decades, one throughline.

Peter is a UX Architect with 20+ years helping government, enterprise, fintech and education organisations turn ambiguous opportunities into clear, tested products and services. He works hands‑on through discovery, user research and rapid prototyping, and leads the teams that do it: from founding and growing a digital experience consultancy, to heading experience design for Singapore's Energy Market Authority, to introducing AI‑assisted delivery practices at NTT DATA that cut turnaround time by 25%. He does his best work close to the craft, framing the right problems, testing ideas with users and mentoring designers and analysts along the way.
