Supporting hook: a two-line Singlish prompt sits above the primary button in the empty state only — "Check see if your numbers / got strike before!" — disappears once numbers are saved.

Sure you want to remove this number ah?

Active-state feedback — every primary/secondary button darkens and insets its shadow on :active with a 1px translateY, giving tactile "pressed" confirmation without relying on colour alone.

Copy Voice — Singlish & Mandarin

Singlish Particles — English Register
Uncle Cheng's voice is warm, direct, and unhurried — like chatting with a kopitiam uncle. Use particles purposefully, not randomly.
leh — soft, slightly resigned or quizzical. "No numbers saved yet leh."
lah — declarative, familiar. "Every number confirm got history lah."
lor — matter-of-fact, resigned. "Key in and save lor. Simple."
ah — questioning, nudging. "How many years of past results you want to check?"
hor — rhetorical, seeking agreement. "...cannot tell you what comes next hor."
sia — mild surprise or emphasis. "This number never struck before sia."
shiok — delight/satisfaction. "System 7 — 7 numbers. Shiok!"
confirm — absolute certainty. "This number confirm struck before."
wah — exclamation. "Wah, almost done…" / "Wah, long time no see."
faster — urgency without aggression. "Faster add your numbers."
Mandarin Particles — 华语口语
Mandarin copy uses the same warm, informal register — the kind Chinese-educated Singaporeans use in WeChat messages. Particles carry the same emotional weight as their Singlish counterparts.
咧 (le) — equivalent of "leh" — soft, slightly resigned. "还没存号码咧。"
啦 (lā) — equivalent of "lah" — declarative, warm. "每个号码都有历史啦。"
咯 (lo) — equivalent of "lor" — matter-of-fact. "输入然后存咯，简单。"
哦 (ó) — equivalent of "ah" — questioning. "你要查几年的记录哦？"
呵 (he) — equivalent of "hor" — seeking agreement. "...告诉不了你下次的呵。"
哇 (wā) — exclamation, same as "wah". "哇，好久没出现了。"
爽 (shuǎng) — equivalent of "shiok" — feels great. "系统7 — 7个号码。爽！"
NCPG / responsible play: always in standard Mandarin with no particles — never casual.

Feedback & Guardrails

Unread Notification Badge
Trigger: any notification with the unread class present
Hamburger icon: 12px solid gold dot (#E8A93C), top-right corner, 2px paper-coloured ring for contrast against the icon glyph
Menu drawer row: 10px gold dot inline after the "Notifications" label, before the chevron
Mark-as-read: fires when the user navigates away from the Notifications screen — via its own back arrow, or the drawer's home icon — not the instant it opens, so the unread state stays visible while actually reading
Unread vs Read Notification Card
Unread: warm gold-tinted fill (#FBF3E1) · 3px gold left border · bold title (700) · small gold dot before title text
Read: plain white card, standard 2px ink border on all sides (no special override needed) · regular-weight title (500) in Ink Soft — recedes so unread items pop by contrast
Rationale: three simultaneous signals (colour + weight + accent) rather than one, so it reads clearly even at a glance
Delete Confirmation
Trigger: tapping delete on a saved 4D/TOTO number never removes it instantly — a modal opens first
Copy: "Sure you want to remove this number ah?" (EN) · "确定要删除这个号码吗？" (ZH) — a direct confirming question, distinct from the app's usual declarative "lah" register, appropriate for a destructive action
Actions: Keep (dismiss, no change) / Delete (removes the entry) — entry is untouched until Delete is explicitly pressed
